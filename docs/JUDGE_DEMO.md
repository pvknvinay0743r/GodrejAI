# Judge Demo Script

## 90-second flow

### 0–10s — Problem
“Warehouse CCTV records events, but supervisors need early warning when handling behaviour can cause damage.”

### 10–25s — AI perception
Upload the controlled warehouse clip.

Show:
- people
- products
- persistent track IDs
- live/near-real-time progress

### 25–45s — Behaviour intelligence
Show a clearly staged risky scenario.

The system should surface:
- behaviour
- exact source timestamp
- risk level
- evidence frame
- observed motion/relationship

Do not narrate a behaviour that the system did not detect.

### 45–60s — Prevention
Open the evidence and show the recommended intervention.

Frame the result as:
**potential risk detected early → human intervention → damage prevention**

### 60–75s — Supervisor assistant
Ask:
“What are the most common risky behaviours?”

The assistant returns only evidence-backed findings and timestamps.

### 75–90s — Auditability
Show:
- annotated replay
- analysis JSON
- risk rationale
- responsible-AI statement

## Winning principle

The strongest demonstration is not the one with the most alerts.

It is the one with the clearest evidence that the system:
1. understands a sequence,
2. avoids noisy duplicate alerts,
3. explains why the behaviour is risky,
4. enables early intervention,
5. and can be measured honestly.
